# Lead designer story and world reference

Recorded from material supplied by the user on September 13, 2026, attributed to
the story's lead creative designer. This is future creative direction, not a

The [verbatim original](lead-designer-original.txt) is preserved without corrections

## World, relationships, and central stakes

- **Recyke:** opening city location, home to the hidden Moral Code meeting place
  and a Nexus Information Hub. Jessie is associated with Recyke. The source also
  spells it “recycle” and “Recycle.”
- **Skyview:** rich neighborhood, written “Sky-view” and “sky view” in the source.
  Janey comes from affluence and power; her father works for Nexus. Her parents'
  house is here. Her father disapproves of her association with Jessie.
- **The Moral Code:** group Janey joins through an oath ritual led by a hooded
  figure. Its underground room is well kept and built from recycled parts, with
  leaking pipes, puddles, flickering news holograms, program monitors, and observers.
  Its wall symbol is still to be determined.
- **Nexus:** opposing power with information infrastructure, security personnel,
  robots, and flight drones. The oath uses the spelling “Nexis.”
- **The code:** recruits swear to do no harm unless absolutely necessary, uphold
  the code despite its burden, and upload it if given access to destroy the Nexis.
  The source does not explain the code's technical operation.
- **Key causal chain:** Janey leaves the module behind during First Strike;
  Nexus forces consequently track Jessie's location and attack Recyke. Janey's
  rescue attempt ends with Jessie being shot and killed. She attacks the regional
  server and ultimately vows to continue fighting in his memory.

## Episode I scene sequence

### 1. Intro

Cutscene: Jessie and Janey walk and banter along a main street in Recyke, then turn
into an alley to a concealed entrance. They give a password; multiple locks turn
and the door opens. As they enter and the music peaks, the camera rises to the city
skyline and opening title credits fade into frame.

### 2. The Moral Code

Cutscene: the pair descend in an old, rusty elevator; Jessie comforts Janey. They
enter the underground room described above. The hooded figure calls, “Recruit!
Step forward.” The player walks Janey to him, and he conducts the oath ritual.
Janey explicitly answers the first two vows; the music swells before the final
answer and the lyrics imply acceptance. She is accepted into the Moral Code.

During the song, a **proposed** training sequence could introduce controls, map
items and their uses, platform jumping, blocking, shooting, and basic mechanics.
After the tasks, the song ends as on the record, with the leader saying,
“Welcome Recruit, to The Moral Code!” Exact vow wording is in the original.

### 3. First Strike

Jessie has installed a device in the Recyke Nexus Information Hub's main operating
system to disable electronic cooling and cause the building to explode. Alarms
sound and Janey reviews mission objectives.

- **Jessie route:** fight through several rooms, disable secondary system controls
  before time expires, and fight a head of security before exiting.
- **Janey route:** fight through other rooms to remove “the module” before time
  expires. A head of security attacks before she reaches it, forcing her to flee
  and leave it behind. She tries several doors to find the only unlocked one,
  reunites with Jessie in another hallway, and follows his directions to escape.

They reach their cruiser in a wrecking yard down the street. The Hub explodes as
they take off. Jessie tells Janey to hide for a few days at her parents' house in
Skyview. The source points to album track 3 for exact dialogue.

### 4. Breaking news in sky view

At her father's house party, Janey's father talks down to her about spending time
with Jessie from Recyke. Another guest asks for the television news. A newscaster
announces an impending Nexus drone/robot attack on Recyke: anyone remaining will
die, and uninvolved parties should leave. The attack follows from the module left
behind in chapter 3. The player helps Janey run to her cruiser. The source refers
to the album track for the father/daughter dialogue.

### 5. Reaching Out

The player flies Janey's cruiser toward Recyke to rescue Jessie, destroys Nexus
flight drones, and avoids hits. Flying through propaganda hologram signs provides
an extra boost. Flying through gun power-ups grants new cruiser weapons.

### 6. Untitled in the supplied outline

Cutscene: Jessie works at a computer with other code operatives in a multistory
building. A missile strikes and the explosion throws him. After he regains
consciousness, the player guides him to an exposed hole where a wall used to be,
avoiding detection by invading robots. Janey arrives in her cruiser and calls to
him. Jessie reveals himself and runs toward her, but is shot and killed. Janey
flies away to escape.

### 7. Ctrl Alt Del

The player flies Janey's cruiser to the regional server, shooting enemy drones
and avoiding hits. As in chapter 5, propaganda hologram signs supply boosts and
fly-through gun power-ups provide new weapons.

### 8. Aquire this!

Outside the regional server, Janey climbs a fence and strikes a robot from behind
with a bat before entering. Inside, she fights toward the regional server hub.

### 9. No separate title supplied

Janey hacks the regional server. The player helps her escape before the timer
expires and the building explodes, fighting robots and drones. Power-ups include
guns stolen from robots. These guns have limited ammunition, requiring the player
to discard spent weapons and steal replacements from downed robots.

### 10. Never say Die

Cutscene and end credits: Janey stands on a rooftop overlooking the city and vows
to Jessie's memory that she will continue the fight against Nexus.

### Janey's abilities and equipment

- Hand grenades and a laser pistol.
- Hologram wristwatch summons a holographic copy for **30 seconds** to double
  damage. Killing an unspecified **X** enemies fills its recharge bar.
- Backpack holds an unspecified **X** items, including health refills and the
  previously listed items.
- Leveling unlocks abilities and gadgets and increases backpack capacity. The
  source places this progression note after Janey's equipment; its broader scope
  is not specified.

### Jessie's abilities and equipment

- Short-duration hoverboard use, including ramming multiple enemies.
- Larger laser pistol firing **three successive rounds per trigger pull**.
- Hologram wristwatch emits an energy pulse for **30 seconds**, stunning enemies
  entering its field. Killing an unspecified **X** enemies recharges its bar.

## Differences, dependencies, and open decisions

- **Names:** use Janey when discussing this designer outline. Existing code and
  standards use Jane. The source mostly uses Jessie, with “Jessy” in the ending.
  These are reference mappings, not instructions to rename files or identifiers.
- **Age and appearance:** existing prototype documentation describes the heroes
  as in their twenties; the new direction says approximately 18 and 19. Approved
  Jane artwork currently has a magenta jacket and dark violet ponytail, differing
  from these clothing/color/hair notes. Record the new direction for future work;
  do not silently replace approved art or its standards.
- **Campaign vs. prototype:** existing playable Escape Skyview and Recyke / First
  Shift routes are prototypes, not implementations of this scene sequence. The
  designer's chapter 1 is the Recyke introduction above.
- **Chapter labels:** the existing campaign list calls chapter 6 “The Death of
  Jessie” and chapter 9 “The Countdown Begins.” This source gives no title for 6
  and no separate title for 9. Preserve that distinction and album spellings.
- **Missing album material:** exact track 3 dialogue, the chapter 4 conversation,
  lyrics, and detailed musical timing have not been supplied with these notes.
  Do not invent them or claim they have been checked against the recording.
- **Unspecified design values:** Moral Code symbol, password, full training
  design, module identity/function, timers, recharge kill counts, backpack
  capacity, progression thresholds, hoverboard duration, and tattoo details.
- **Tentative choices:** chapter 2 training is suggested (“could”); Janey's
  jeans/skirt are alternatives and shoulder armor has a question mark. Character
  route selection in chapter 3 is described conditionally, without specifying a
  complete selection or switching system.
