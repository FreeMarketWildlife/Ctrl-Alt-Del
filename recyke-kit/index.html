03 / PRODUCTION RECORD
Native. Modular. Isolated.
What was reused
Recyke's native palette, cargo crates, refuse-bin group, sorting press and boiler/steam. The train and FIX lettering were separated into modular native pieces. Source paths and hashes are recorded per asset.
What is new
Adjacency-based street pieces, modular window and shutter bays, roof/corner/recess modules, concealed door states, pipe connectors, fan, cables, lamps, scrap, notices and reflections. Big and mini have independent dimensions and scene layouts.
What remains open
Exact password, Moral Code emblem, lock sounds and choreography, dialogue, and the underground/elevator kit. The hidden entrance is the end of this study. Big characters retain their historical approved outfits unchanged.
